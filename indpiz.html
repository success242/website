Our Story

Not just pizza.An experience.

De_livvi began with a simple obsession: pizza good enough to travel across Lagos for. We hand-stretch every base, let the dough rest overnight, and finish each one in a blazing wood-fired oven — the only way to get that blistered crust and whisper of smoke.

Our ingredients are chosen, never bought at random. Whole mozzarella torn by hand, sun-ripened tomatoes, basil picked the same morning. Small batches, made fresh daily, because shortcuts taste like shortcuts.
